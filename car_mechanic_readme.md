# 🏎️ Car Mechanic & Parts Shop Simulator

Hey! Thanks for checking out my game. This is a text-based garage management simulator built completely in Python using Streamlit. I wanted to build a game where you have total control over buying inventory, managing your cash, and setting your own prices for car parts to see how customers react.

## 🌟 What is this project?

You start out as an independent mechanic running a fresh garage with 50 gold to your name. Your goal is to build up your business, balance your stock of car components, and stay profitable over time.

Every day you open the shop, a randomized group of customers walk in looking for specific parts. Every customer comes with their own hidden budget—if you price your parts fairly, you make a sale and stack up gold; if you price them too high, they'll leave empty-handed!

## ⚙️ How the Game Works

* **The Parts Shop:** Spend your hard-earned gold to stock up on basic components like maintenance kits and dashboards, or invest heavily in big-ticket items like engines, gearboxes, car bodies, and custom build parts.

* **Pricing & Inventory Control:** Head over to the garage to view your current inventory levels. You can adjust the selling price for every single item on your shelves whenever you want.

* **Daily Customer Flow:** Click **Start Day** to advance time and simulate customer visits. Each customer gets procedurally generated budgets depending on the part's value.

* **Garage Log & Stats:** Check your dynamic garage log to review recent sales, missed opportunities, and purchases. You can also view your overall stats to see your total customer count and day progress.

## 🛠️ Built With

* **Python 3.x**

* **Streamlit** (for the UI layout, interactive inputs, and session state tracking)

* **Random Module** (for procedural customer visits and dynamic budgets)

## 🚀 How to Run the Game

1. **Download the File:** Grab `car_mechanic.py` from this repository or download the source code as a `.zip` file.

2. **Install Dependencies:** Open your terminal or command prompt and install Streamlit:

   ```
   pip install streamlit
   
   ```

3. **Launch the Game:** Navigate to the folder containing your code and run:

   ```
   streamlit run car_mechanic.py
   
   ```
